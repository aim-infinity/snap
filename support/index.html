Support

We’re here to help.

Questions, bugs or ideas: email us or send feedback from the editor. We read every message.

Contact

Email iaimforinfinity@gmail.com. Snap is made by Infinity Labs.

Send feedback from Snap

Open the editor and click Send feedback in the top bar. Pick a heart rating, write a note and press Send Feedback. Only your rating, message, the time, the extension version and the screen name are sent; see the privacy policy.

Report a capture issue

For a capture issue, include your Chrome version, extension version, and what happened. A screenshot is optional; remove sensitive content before sending it. Up to 120 steps or approximately 200 MB of image data per flow, subject to available storage. Full page capture is limited to 30 viewport tiles, 30,000 pixels in height, and 64 million output pixels. Dynamic pages or nested scroll areas may require visible-area or selected-area capture.

Install or update

Install Snap from the Chrome Web Store. Chrome updates it automatically. To check your version, open chrome://extensions and look at Snap’s card.

Keep your flows safe

Flows are stored only in your Chrome profile. Export anything important (ZIP or PDF) before removing the extension or clearing browser data.
