How it works

Three steps from page to shareable flow

  1. 01

    Start a flow

    Open Snap on any website, name the journey, and press Start Snapping. A small toolbar appears on the page.

  2. 02

    Snap what matters

    Capture the visible area, drag to select a region, or stitch the full page. Press ⌥⇧S without leaving the page.

  3. 03

    Review and export

    Reorder steps, add notes, annotate or redact, then export PNG, ZIP or PDF, or paste the flow straight into Figma.

Features

Everything a flow needs, nothing it doesn't

  • Three capture modes

    Visible area, a selected rectangle, or the full page stitched automatically, with fixed headers shown once.

  • Annotate and redact

    Rectangles, arrows and text call out what matters. Redact pixelates sensitive areas in every export. Originals stay untouched.

  • Organize the story

    Drag or use the keyboard to reorder, rename steps, add descriptions, and restore anything you remove.

  • Export anywhere

    Single PNGs at any width, a numbered ZIP with flow notes, print-ready PDF, or copy the whole flow into Figma.

  • Keyboard first

    A fixed shortcut to snap, arrow keys through steps, and shortcuts for every annotation tool.

  • Light and dark

    A calm editor in both themes, following your system or your choice, with a toolbar that stays out of your screenshots.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Snap record video?

No. Snap only saves the screenshots you take, one step at a time. There are no automatic captures when you click, scroll or navigate.

Where are my flows stored?

In your Chrome profile on this device, encrypted. Export anything important before removing the extension or clearing browser data.

Can I hide sensitive information?

Yes. Draw a redaction over any area and it is pixelated in every PNG, ZIP, PDF and Figma export. Your original capture is kept unchanged in the editor.

Does it work on every website?

On normal web pages, yes. Browser-internal pages and incognito tabs are excluded. When you move to a new site, Chrome may ask you to open Snap again to grant access.

How long can a flow be?

Up to 120 steps or about 200 MB per flow. Full-page captures stitch up to 30 screens.
