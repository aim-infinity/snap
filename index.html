The problem

Your screenshots have a story. Stop losing it in Downloads.

A screenshot shortcut gives you files named after timestamps, scattered next to invoices and installers. Snap gives you a flow: every capture numbered, titled and kept with its URL, in the order you took it.

How it works

From live website to shareable flow in three steps

  1. 01

    Name the flow

    Open Snap on any website, name what you are documenting, and press Start Snapping. A small toolbar joins the page.

  2. 02

    Snap only what matters

    Click through as usual and snap the visible area, a selected region or the full page. Or press ⌥⇧S without leaving the page.

  3. 03

    Mark it up and ship it

    Reorder steps, add notes, annotate or redact. Then paste into Figma, download a PDF, or export PNGs.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Snap free?

Yes. Install it from the Chrome Web Store and start snapping. There is no account and no sign-up.

How is Snap different from a screen recorder?

Snap never records video and never captures on its own. You decide which screens become steps, and each one is a sharp screenshot you can annotate, redact and export.

How does Copy to Figma work?

Copy to Figma puts the whole flow on your clipboard. Paste into any Figma file and each step appears as a numbered, labeled frame, laid out left to right. There is no plugin and no Figma account connection.

Can I share flows with my team?

Snap has no cloud workspace, by design. Share what you export: a PDF, a ZIP of numbered PNGs with your notes, a single PNG, or a paste into a shared Figma file.

Where are my flows stored?

In your Chrome profile on this device, encrypted. Export anything important before removing the extension or clearing browser data.

Can I hide sensitive information?

Yes. Draw a redaction over any area and it is pixelated in every PNG, ZIP, PDF and Figma export. Your original capture is kept unchanged in the editor.

Does it work on every website?

On normal web pages, yes. Browser-internal pages and incognito tabs are excluded. If you allow access to all sites when you start a flow, the toolbar follows you to new tabs, other sites and sign-in windows. Without it, open Snap or press the shortcut on each new tab to continue the flow. See how it works.

Why does Snap ask for access to all sites?

So a flow can continue wherever you go, for example when an app opens a new tab or a sign-in window. Snap only shows its toolbar there and still captures only when you click Snap. It never reads or uploads page content. The access is optional: decline and Snap works tab by tab, or remove it anytime in Chrome’s extension settings.

How long can a flow be?

Up to 120 steps or about 200 MB per flow. Full-page captures stitch up to 30 screens.

Does Snap work on my phone?

No. Snap is a Chrome extension for desktop. Open this page on your computer to install it.
