Screenshot to PDF: A Free Chrome Extension to Turn Web Pages into One PDF

Stop pasting screenshots into a document one by one. Here is how to capture pages in Chrome and export them as a single, readable PDF in a few minutes.

By Infinity Labs · October 23, 2026 · 4 min read

You need to send someone a walkthrough: a bug, a checkout flow, the steps to change a setting. The usual route is a pile of screenshots pasted into a document, each one resized, labeled and nudged into place.

The faster way: install a screenshot extension that collects your captures in order and exports them as one PDF. Snap is a free Chrome extension that does exactly that. Each screenshot becomes its own page, with a title, your note, the page’s URL and any annotations you added.

Disclosure: we make Snap. It is free, needs no account and keeps your screenshots on your device.

How to turn screenshots into a PDF in Chrome

1. Install Snap and start a flow

Add Snap from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to the toolbar. Open the website you want to capture, click the Snap icon, give the flow a name such as “Change billing address”, and click Start Snapping.

New to Snap? The getting started guide walks through the first flow in more detail.

2. Capture each screen

A small toolbar appears at the bottom of the page. Click Snap, or press Option+Shift+S on a Mac (Alt+Shift+S on Windows), each time the screen shows something worth keeping. Snap never captures on its own, so you decide what goes into the PDF.

Pick the right capture mode from the toolbar:

  • Visible area for what is on screen now.
  • Selected area to focus on one panel or error message.
  • Full page to capture the whole scrolling page as one image.

3. Review, annotate and redact

Click Finish to open the editor. For each step, write a short title and a one-line description; these appear on the PDF page above the screenshot. Drag thumbnails to reorder, and remove any duplicates.

Use the annotation tools to box the important button or add an arrow, and pixelate anything private. Our guide to redacting screenshots safely covers what to hide.

4. Export the PDF

  1. Choose an export size next to the flow name if you want smaller images. Original size keeps every pixel.
  2. Click Download PDF. Chrome’s print dialog opens with one page per step.
  3. Set Destination to Save as PDF and click Save. The file is named after your flow.

Each page shows the flow name, “Step 3 / 8”, your title and description, the screenshot with annotations, and a clickable link to the source page.

Other ways to save screenshots as a PDF

  • Print a single page to PDF. Chrome’s print dialog can save the current page as a PDF. It is fine for an article, but it reflows the layout and does not capture states such as open menus.
  • Combine image files. On a Mac, select the images in Finder and use Quick Actions, then Create PDF. On Windows, select the images and print them to “Microsoft Print to PDF”. You still have to name, order and annotate the images yourself.
  • Paste into a document. Flexible, but slow for more than a few screens, and the screenshots are easy to get out of order.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save multiple screenshots as one PDF in Chrome?

Install Snap, start a flow and click Snap on each screen. Click Finish, then Download PDF, and choose Save as PDF in Chrome’s print dialog. Every screenshot becomes its own page.

Is there a free Chrome extension to convert screenshots to PDF?

Yes. Snap is free in the Chrome Web Store with no account or paid tier, and PDF export is included.

Can I add notes and arrows to the screenshots before exporting?

Yes. Give each step a title and description, and add boxes, arrows, text or pixelation in the editor. They are all included in the PDF.

Can I capture a full scrolling page into the PDF?

Yes. Choose Full page in the toolbar. Snap scrolls and stitches the page into one image, up to 30 screens or 30,000 pixels tall.

Are my screenshots uploaded to make the PDF?

No. Snap builds the PDF in your browser and stores flows in your Chrome profile. Nothing is uploaded.

How many screenshots can one PDF have?

A flow holds up to 120 steps, so the PDF can have up to 120 pages.
