How to

How to take a full page screenshot in Chrome

  1. 01

    Open Snap on the page

    Click the Snap icon in your toolbar, name the flow and press Start Snapping. The Snap toolbar appears on the page.

  2. 02

    Choose Full page

    Open the capture menu on the toolbar and pick Full page. Visible area and Selected area are there too.

  3. 03

    Press Snap

    Snap scrolls from top to bottom, stitches the screens into one image and returns you to where you were. Press Escape to cancel.

  4. 04

    Save or share it

    Download the step as a PNG, export the flow as a PDF or ZIP, or paste it into Figma. Add annotations first if you need to.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I take a full page screenshot in Chrome?

Install Snap, open it on the page and press Start Snapping. In the toolbar’s capture menu choose Full page, then press Snap. Snap scrolls and stitches the page into one image you can export as a PNG or PDF.

How long can a full page screenshot be?

Up to 30 screens, 30,000 pixels tall and 64 million pixels in total per capture.

Does it repeat sticky headers?

No. Fixed and sticky content is shown once, at the top of the capture, instead of on every screen.

Does it work on every page?

On most pages, yes. Infinite feeds, pages that change height while scrolling, nested scrolling apps and wide horizontal layouts may need Visible area or Selected area instead. Browser-internal pages and incognito tabs are not supported.

Is it free, and are my screenshots uploaded?

Snap is free, with no account. Captures are stored encrypted in your Chrome profile and nothing is uploaded.
