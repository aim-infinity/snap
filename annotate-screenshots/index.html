How to

How to annotate a screenshot in Chrome

  1. 01

    Snap the page

    Open Snap on any website and press Snap, or ⌥⇧S. Capture the visible area, a selected region or the full page.

  2. 02

    Open the editor

    Press Finish. Your captures open in order in the Snap editor, ready to mark up.

  3. 03

    Pick a tool and draw

    Rectangle R, Arrow A, Text T and Redact B. Select and move with V, undo with ⌘Z.

  4. 04

    Export or paste

    Download a PNG, a ZIP or a PDF, copy a single step, or paste the whole flow into Figma. Annotations are drawn into every export.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I annotate a screenshot in Chrome?

Install Snap, open it on the page and press Snap. Press Finish to open the editor, then use Rectangle (R), Arrow (A), Text (T) or Redact (B) to mark up the capture. Export a PNG or PDF, or paste the flow into Figma.

Can I blur or hide sensitive information?

Yes. The Redact tool pixelates any area you draw over, and the pixelation is applied in every PNG, ZIP, PDF and Figma export. Pixelation hides details from casual viewing but is not a guarantee, so avoid capturing passwords or secrets at all.

Are my original screenshots changed?

No. Annotations are stored separately with each step, so the original capture stays untouched in the editor. You can move, edit or delete annotations at any time.

Can I annotate a whole flow, not just one screenshot?

Yes. Every step in a flow can have its own boxes, arrows, text and redactions, and the whole annotated flow exports together as a PDF, a ZIP or a Figma paste.

Is Snap’s annotation tool free?

Yes. Snap is free in the Chrome Web Store, with no account and no sign-up. Your screenshots and annotations stay in your Chrome profile.
